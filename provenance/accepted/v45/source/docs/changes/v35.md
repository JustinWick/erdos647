## PNT dependency: retained proof path, not an allegedly completed catalogue

`Erdos647Research.Compat.PNTPlus` now imports only `Mathlib` and
`PrimeNumberTheoremAnd.MediumPNT`. It no longer imports the broad
`IEANTN.RosserSchoenfeld.RosserSchoenfeldPrime` module.

Seven already-proved PNT/integrability routines are extracted from that pinned
upstream file into the compatibility boundary. They retain their names, types,
and proof arguments; their origin is recorded in
`provenance/pntplus/v35/source-map.json`. The downstream adapters still consume
exactly the same quantitative theta bound and integrability statement.

This is **not** a claim to have proved the nine unimplemented statements in
`ZetaSummary`, the two unused Wiener decay statements, the Li(2) certificate
interfaces, or every result in the PNT+ repository. Those unrelated catalogues
are excluded from our active import graph. The installed upstream checkout is
unchanged and still contains its original unfinished files.

A new independent `pntplus_inputs` gate does BOTH:

1. Recursively scans all PNT+ source files reachable from the compatibility
   boundary. A placeholder anywhere in this retained source subtree fails the
   gate, even if the final theta theorem does not use it. Missing source, a
   reintroduced IEANTN/Wiener/LeanCert branch, or unexpected external roots also
   fail. Exact inspected sources and hashes are included in results.
2. Builds the boundary and audits `MediumPNT` plus all seven extracted routines.
   The two consumed inputs also have expanded statement checks. Standard axioms
   remain `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` only.

A lexical source scan is not a substitute for the kernel/theorem audit. Neither
check alone is used to claim the whole upstream repository is complete.

## Lint repairs

The reported unused simp arguments, ineffective `change`/`ring`, unnecessary
`<;>` combinators, and deprecated `push_neg` are removed or replaced with their
non-deprecated equivalents. No linter is disabled. All existing theorem statements,
including the signed budget and natural quotient definitions, are preserved.

Every project library now builds with `-DwarningAsError=true`, including ordinary
Lake builds. The runner also rejects warnings replayed from cached build logs.
It still runs each selected exact-type/axiom audit; a cached build does not replace
those checks. Numerical bounds and proof hypotheses are not changed by lint work.

## Actual proof error

`p : ℕ`. The target still compares real division with the real cast of the NATURAL
quotient `H / p`, not a real quotient substituted for that integer quotient.
